Total Recall

People remember more if material is meaningful.

Verbatim vs. Gist
Passage: If the balloons popped, the sound would not be able to carry.

P17: If the balloons popped, the sound would not be able to carry.
P21: If the balloons pop, the sound won’t reach the window.

Meaning leads to gist recall; nonsense leads to verbatim recall.

Inference intrusions
Passage: The human voice is not loud enough to carry that far.

P23: But his voice won't be loud enough to reach the window.
Passage: A break in the middle of the wire would also cause
problems.
P21: He also needs a good flow of electricity to the speaker.
People remember inferences made while reading a passage.
