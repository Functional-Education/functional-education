CS-411: 6 credits
• Written exam (50%): we will make a past example available on Moodle
• Project (50%):
– Teams of 3 people
– Select a topic that is difficult to understand
– Design learning activities with and without AI affordance
– Run an experiment and evaluate it
– There will be intermediate mandatory milestones
– Present the final project on December 15 (in-person attendance mandatory)
– 20% initial draft (M3), 20% plan + working conditions (M4), 30% poster
presentation (M6), 30% final report (M7)

Detailed instructions on Moodle

The project
• Design a lesson with and without AI affordances
• We expect you to use generative AI as part of your project work,
including to support coding and other project tasks.
– You remain responsible for all content and work you submit.
– You must be able to justify your design decisions and explain all
choices, analyses, and results.

• Document your interactions with generative AI. We will use these
interactions throughout the semester to reflect on how AI can
support—or hinder—learning and work, connecting your own
experiences to the theories discussed in class.

The objectives
• You will learn to:
– Define specific, measurable learning goals
– Apply learning theories to design activities
– Implement an AI-supported digital lesson
– Design and execute an experiment comparing AI-support with a non-AI
version.
– Analyze data quantitatively and qualitatively
– Collaborate effectively with AI
– Analyze how different ways of using AI can support or hinder learning
– Critically reflect on your own use of AI for learning

Your Experience

2• What tools are you using and how are you using
